Hosted beta: privacy

Effective 2026-11-04. Operator: HZHLLC. Contact: privacy@piibuddy.com.

This page covers only the invited-tester hosted API at mcp.piibuddy.com. The PII Buddy desktop app and command line run on your own computer and do not send your documents anywhere unless you turn on optional cloud verification.

Send synthetic data only

The beta is for testing. Use made-up names, emails, numbers and addresses. Do not send real personal information, client data or anything confidential. Detection is not complete: some names, addresses, phone formats and IDs are known to be missed, so output can still contain personal information.

What happens to your text

  • Your request travels over HTTPS. Encryption ends at our server, so the server sees your text in plain form while it processes it. This is not end-to-end encryption.
  • The text is processed in memory and the answer is sent back. We do not write request bodies, your text or the returned mapping to disk or to our application logs, and we do not keep a copy after the response.
  • The mapping that links placeholders such as <<EMAIL_1>> to the original values is returned to you and not stored by us.
  • We do not use your text to train models, and we do not sell or share it. No third-party AI service sees it in this beta.

What is logged

  • Application logs record, per request: a random request ID, the route, the status, the time taken, your tester ID, the request size in bytes, the number of items found, and an error code. Never the text.
  • The web proxy and hosting platform in front of the service may record your IP address, the time and the URL path. Logs are rotated and capped in size.
  • Rate-limit counters are kept in memory and reset when the service restarts.

Where it runs

On a virtual server rented from Hostinger that also runs other services. It is not certified for HIPAA, GDPR or any other compliance scheme; do not use it where such certification is required.

Your choices

Stop using your tester key at any time, or ask us to revoke it. Questions or requests: privacy@piibuddy.com.
